\section{Tensor products as adjoint functors}

\subsection{Background on adjoint functors}

Adjoint functors are a generalization of the concept of ``inverse functions," where $f\circ g=1_Y$ implies $f$ is $g$'s left-inverse and $g\circ f=1_X$ implies $g$ is $f$'s. Similarly, for functors $F:\mathcal{C}_1\to\mathcal{C}_2$ and $G:\mathcal{C}_2\to\mathcal{C}_1$, we say $F,G$ are left and right \emph{adjoints} if for all $X\in\mathcal{C}_1,Y\in\mathcal{C}_2$,
\begin{align*}
    \Phi_{X,Y}:\Hom_{\mathcal{C}_2}(F(X),Y)
        \xrightarrow{\sim}\Hom_{\mathcal{C}_1}(X,G(Y)).
\end{align*}
These are bijections of sets, \emph{natural in both $X$ and $Y$}.
Explicitly, for $a:X'\to X$, $b:Y\to Y'$, and $h:F(X)\to Y$,
\begin{align*}
    \Phi_{X',Y'}\bigl(b\circ h\circ F(a)\bigr)
        =G(b)\circ\Phi_{X,Y}(h)\circ a.
\end{align*}
